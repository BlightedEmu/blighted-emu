## Open after round 4

### The 41 reader failures after a vitals member

**Tried.** Product decode of all 176 failures. 32 are class 13 slot bits, 8 are class 11 counts above the client limit, 1 is a class 11 flag. The vitals member before them uses slot byte 1 and the 14-child table.

**Tried.** On the 32 position failures, clear every slot bit except bit 0 and read again. 17 then read. 15 still fail. 8 of the slot bytes have bit 0 clear.

**Tried.** A search that changes one child width from 0 through 40, or adds a pad of 1 through 48 bytes, and asks the rest of the payload to be only known classes. Many widths succeed together on the same body, because a type 11 body can absorb the shift. The search does not pick one child. Log: `probe-summary-widths.txt`.

**Why it is open.** No one width, and no pad, is tied to one child for all 41, and the position mask does not clear the 15 that still fail. The client image does not show a second size for children 9 or 13 (`FUN_1432d9460` is 4 bytes, `FUN_142a42e10` is 2 bytes).

### The 57 vitals truncations after type 11

**Tried.** The product vitals reader on the body after the accepted type 11 member. It truncates on 57 of 57. The failure file splits them into 37, 15, and 5 by bytes needed and bytes left, as in the table above.

**Tried.** The same 14 widths in a second walker on that body. It reports a finished body of 5 to 16 bytes on all 57 and does not name a child. The two walks disagree, so the short child is not identified. The slot byte is 1, so the tail is not slot 1.

**Why it is open.** The framed payload ends inside a field the product reader is still reading. The second walker does not stop in that field. A width is not assigned.

### Class 3451

**Tried.** Constructor `FUN_1433d8bf0`: slot 0, plus five appended slots. Names and which slot each name uses are in the component note. Two leader position readers were sized (3 bytes and 10 bytes).

**Tried.** Header scan of the slot byte and of the child mask when the slot byte is 0 or 1. Slot byte 1 is 103,935 bodies. No slot byte in the top 16 is a 99.9% share of the 908,890 reached bodies. The largest child mask on slot byte 0 or 1 is 10,792.

**Why it is open.** The body uses more than one slot, and the child readers other than the two leader positions were not sized, so an end-to-end fit was not run.
